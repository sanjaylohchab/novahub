💡 Cheatsheet

Agar aap fas gaye hain, toh yeh steps follow karein:

  1. 5L wale jug ko poora bharo.
  2. 5L se paani 3L wale mein dalo (Ab 5L wale me 2L bachega).
  3. 3L wale ko poora fenk (Empty) do.
  4. 5L wale me bacha hua 2L paani wapas 3L wale me daal do.
  5. Firse 5L wale ko nalke se poora bhar lo.
  6. Aakhiri baar: 5L wale se 3L wale ko bharo. (3L wale mein pehle se 2L tha, toh wo sirf 1L lega).
  7. BHOOM! 5L wale mein exactly 4L bachega! 🎉
